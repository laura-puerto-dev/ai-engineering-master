# EN-001 — Annual Leave and Time Off Policy

**Northfern Technologies**  
**Policy ID:** EN-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** England Human Resources  
**Applies to:** Employees assigned to Northfern Technologies' England entity  
**Classification:** Internal — available to all employees in England  
**Language:** English

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees in England request and take annual leave, how public holidays and other time off are handled, and where to obtain information about their individual leave balances. It aims to support employee wellbeing while enabling teams to plan adequate operational coverage.

This policy is specific to England. Employees assigned to other countries must consult their applicable local policies. Statutory rights and individual contractual terms take precedence wherever they provide greater protection.

## 2. Holiday year and annual entitlement

The holiday year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

Full-time employees working a standard five-day week receive **25 working days of paid annual leave per complete holiday year**, **plus the bank/public holidays applicable to their England work location**. These bank/public holidays are separate from the 25-day entitlement and must not be deducted from it.

The number and dates of bank/public holidays can vary by year. Employees should consult the published company holiday calendar rather than assume that exactly eight bank holidays will fall in every holiday year. Part-time and non-standard working patterns are handled on an appropriate pro-rata or equivalent basis so that statutory entitlements are respected.

## 3. New starters, leavers and changes in working pattern

For employees who join or leave during the holiday year, annual leave is calculated proportionately to the relevant period of employment, subject to applicable law and contractual terms. Changes to contracted working days or hours may also require a recalculation.

The HR system is the authoritative source for an employee's recorded entitlement, adjustments, approved leave and remaining balance. This policy cannot establish an individual's exact balance.

## 4. Requesting annual leave

Employees should ordinarily submit requests **at least 14 calendar days before** the proposed first day of leave through the approved HR system. Requests should include the proposed dates and any information needed for team scheduling, but not unnecessary personal details.

The line manager reviews requests in light of staffing, previously approved absences and business continuity. A request is not approved until the approval is recorded in the designated system. Employees should avoid making non-refundable arrangements before receiving approval.

Short-notice requests may be considered where circumstances justify them. The ordinary notice period is an internal planning expectation and does not override any applicable legal notice rules or statutory rights.

## 5. Consecutive leave and team coverage

Employees may ordinarily request **up to 10 consecutive working days** of annual leave. Longer periods require advance discussion and special approval from the line manager in coordination with England HR.

Managers should apply approval criteria consistently and should not unreasonably prevent employees from taking their leave. Any operational limits must be applied consistently with applicable law, including employees' ability to take their statutory entitlement.

## 6. Carry-over of unused annual leave

As a standard company arrangement, employees may request to carry over **up to 5 unused working days** into the next holiday year, subject to manager and HR confirmation. Normally, approved carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of that following year.

This standard limit and deadline do **not** restrict carry-over rights required by law, including where statutory leave could not be taken because of qualifying sickness absence or statutory family leave, or other legally protected circumstances. HR will assess such cases individually and apply the relevant statutory rules and deadlines.

Employees should plan their leave throughout the year rather than rely on carry-over.

## 7. Company-paid personal days

In addition to annual leave, Northfern Technologies provides **2 paid personal days per full holiday year** to eligible full-time employees in England. These days are a company benefit and are separate from annual leave and bank/public holidays.

Personal days should normally be requested through the HR system and coordinated with the line manager. For urgent circumstances, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably practicable. Personal days are not automatically carried into the following year. Entitlement for part-year employment or non-standard working patterns is administered by HR in accordance with applicable terms.

## 8. Sickness absence

An employee who is unable to work due to illness should notify their line manager as soon as reasonably practicable, following the local sickness-reporting procedure. HR will explain any required self-certification, medical evidence and applicable sick-pay arrangements.

Sickness absence is managed separately from annual leave. If illness affects a period of booked holiday, employees should contact HR so that any applicable rights and adjustments can be assessed. Employees must not be required to disclose a diagnosis to colleagues or include sensitive medical information in ordinary team scheduling records.

## 9. Family-related and emergency leave

Employees may have statutory rights to maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental, parental bereavement, dependants' emergency leave and other forms of protected time off, depending on their circumstances and current law.

Employees should notify their manager and England HR using the relevant procedure. In emergencies, notification should be made as soon as reasonably practicable. HR will determine eligibility, documentation and pay arrangements under the applicable rules; the **2 company-paid personal days** do not replace statutory entitlements.

## 10. Bank holidays and operational requirements

The company publishes an annual calendar identifying the bank/public holidays applicable to England employees. The typical calendar includes around **8 bank holidays**, but the precise number and dates must be checked for the relevant year.

Where business operations require work on a bank/public holiday, the employee's contractual arrangements and applicable legal requirements determine any substitute time off or other treatment. Employees should consult HR rather than assume a particular premium rate or substitute-day arrangement.

## 11. Records, privacy and access

Annual leave requests, approvals, adjustments and individual balances are maintained in the approved HR systems. Employees may access their own records; managers may access information necessary to administer leave for their direct reports; and authorized HR personnel may access records within their responsibilities.

A general policy document does not disclose personal leave balances, individual approvals, medical diagnoses or other confidential employee records. Access and handling of employee information must comply with **GLB-002 — Information Security Policy** and applicable privacy requirements.

## 12. Exceptions, interpretation and related policies

England HR is responsible for interpreting this policy and assessing exceptions, together with the relevant line manager where appropriate. Any exception must be documented and must not reduce statutory rights. Employees should raise questions or discrepancies in their recorded entitlement with England HR.
