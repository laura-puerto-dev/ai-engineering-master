## 1. Purpose

Northfern Technologies is committed to conducting business with integrity, professionalism, and respect. This Code of Conduct sets out common expectations for everyone working for the company across Spain, Mexico, and England. It applies to day-to-day decisions, relationships with colleagues and customers, and the use of company resources.

This policy establishes minimum company-wide standards. Applicable law and relevant local policies must also be followed. Where a local requirement imposes a stricter or mandatory rule, employees must follow that requirement and seek clarification from their local HR team when necessary.

## 2. Respectful and inclusive workplace

All employees must treat colleagues, customers, suppliers, and business partners with dignity and respect. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, intimidation, and retaliation are prohibited.

Employees are expected to communicate professionally, including in remote meetings, messaging tools, and written communications. Concerns about inappropriate conduct should be reported through an authorized company channel.

## 3. Integrity and conflicts of interest

Employees must act honestly and avoid situations in which personal interests could improperly influence professional decisions. Potential conflicts of interest, including relevant outside business activities or personal relationships affecting procurement or hiring decisions, must be disclosed to the appropriate manager or HR contact.

Disclosure does not automatically mean a conflict exists or that an activity is prohibited. The company will review the circumstances and determine any necessary safeguards.

## 4. Gifts, hospitality, and business courtesies

Modest business courtesies may be acceptable when they are lawful, infrequent, transparent, and cannot reasonably be perceived as influencing a business decision.

For the purposes of this fictional corporate policy, the indicative threshold for a gift is **EUR 50 (or the equivalent local-currency value)**. Gifts above this threshold require review by an authorized company representative **before acceptance or offering**. A gift at or below the threshold is not automatically permitted: cash or cash-equivalent gifts, improper inducements, and gifts that create an actual or perceived conflict of interest are prohibited regardless of value.

Employees must consult their local HR or Compliance contact if they are uncertain whether a gift or invitation is appropriate. Applicable local law and customer-specific restrictions take precedence.

## 5. Confidentiality and information handling

Employees must protect confidential information belonging to Northfern Technologies, its customers, suppliers, and employees. Information may only be accessed, used, and shared for authorized business purposes and with appropriate recipients.

Confidentiality obligations apply in the office, at home, while traveling, and after a person's employment ends, to the extent required by applicable agreements and law.

Detailed requirements for devices, access credentials, approved services, incident reporting, and data handling are set out in **GLB-002 — Information Security Policy**.

## 6. Responsible use of company resources

Company devices, systems, software, and communication channels must be used responsibly and in accordance with company policies. Employees must not bypass security controls, share accounts, or install unapproved tools for business use.

Incidental personal use may be permitted where it is reasonable, lawful, and does not interfere with work or violate other company rules. Questions about acceptable use should be directed to the relevant IT or HR contact.

## 7. Artificial intelligence and automated tools

Employees may use AI-assisted tools for business tasks only when those tools and their intended uses have been authorized by the company.

Customer data, personal information, confidential documents, source code, and other restricted material must not be entered into public or unapproved AI services. Approval to use an AI tool does not by itself authorize the disclosure of every category of information to that tool.

AI-generated outputs must be reviewed for accuracy, appropriateness, confidentiality, and potential bias before they are relied upon or shared. Employees remain responsible for their professional decisions and work products.

Refer to **GLB-002 — Information Security Policy** for the applicable technical and information-handling requirements.

## 8. External communications and public statements

Employees must not disclose confidential company or customer information through social media, public presentations, interviews, online forums, or other external channels without authorization.

Only designated representatives may make official statements on behalf of Northfern Technologies. Employees expressing personal opinions must avoid implying that they speak for the company.

## 9. Raising concerns and reporting incidents

Employees who become aware of suspected misconduct, conflicts of interest, inappropriate information sharing, or other possible violations should report the concern promptly through an authorized manager, local HR contact, or designated corporate reporting channel.

For urgent information-security incidents, employees must follow the reporting process described in **GLB-002** rather than waiting for a routine HR review.

Reports should be made in good faith and contain relevant factual information where available. Employees are not expected to investigate allegations themselves or access records beyond their authorization.

## 10. Protection against retaliation

Northfern Technologies prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or cooperates appropriately with an authorized review.

Concerns will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible, subject to the need to investigate, protect individuals, and comply with applicable law. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

## 11. Review of potential violations

Potential violations will be assessed by authorized personnel in accordance with applicable company procedures and local law. Outcomes may include guidance, corrective action, or other proportionate measures, depending on the facts and applicable requirements.

Managers must not independently access restricted HR records or disclose sensitive case details merely because a concern involves a member of their team. Employees should refer procedural questions to the relevant local HR or Compliance contact.

## 12. Local requirements, interpretation, and related documents

This Code applies across the company's operations in **Spain, Mexico, and England**. It does not replace mandatory local legal requirements, employment agreements, or applicable country-specific policies.

Questions about interpretation or local application should be directed to the employee's local HR team. A policy applying in one country does not automatically apply to employees assigned to another country.
